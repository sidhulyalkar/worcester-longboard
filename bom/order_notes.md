# Worcester X1 Alpha purchasing notes

> **HISTORICAL v0.1 REFERENCE ONLY.** Do not purchase from this list. The current purchasing authority is `hardware/procurement_manifest.json` plus the dated procurement snapshot. The TRAMPA/14S path below is retained for design history and comparison.

## Compatibility-defining mechanical order
1. TRAMPA 35° HS11 9-69 deck, factory drilled. Select ply from the current manufacturer Flexometer and intended ride feel.
2. Front hydraulic-brake truck configured for the selected 5-spoke wheel family.
3. Rear twin spur gear drive + channel truck + motors configured for 9/63 (7:1) helical steel gears and 6376 160 KV motors.
4. Four 8-inch SUPERSTAR-family 5-spoke wheels, 12 mm mountainboard axle setup.
5. Ratchet bindings + heel straps.

## Motor control
6. Two housed VESC 6/75 controllers, one per rear motor.
7. VESC WAND + compatible NRF receiver.

## Battery
8. Professionally assembled 14S4P P50B module to X1 electrical/envelope specification.
9. Pack-builder-selected BMS with 14S support, balancing, multiple temperature channels and useful telemetry.
10. Matched charger with daily-charge and full-charge setpoints defined by the pack builder.
11. DC-rated main fuse, precharge/anti-spark and service disconnect sized for the final pack and conductor system.

## X1 electronics
12. ESP32-S3 development ECU.
13. 3.3 V-compatible physical CAN transceiver.
14. Two front digital Hall sensors plus magnets.
15. Low-noise 6-axis IMU.
16. Optional GNSS for trail logging.
17. High-endurance microSD + interface.
18. Waterproof sensor connectors, twisted-pair CAN cable, abrasion sleeve and strain relief.

## Custom fabrication
