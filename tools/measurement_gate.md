# Measurements required after the first mechanical order arrives

Record these before custom parts go to final machining.

1. center deck width at each battery mounting-ear X-coordinate
2. deck thickness at those locations
3. deck neutral crown and loaded deflection under intended stance
4. nearest existing deck-hole coordinates relative to board centerline
5. rear motor/gear housing envelope at maximum steering angle
6. front brake hose envelope at maximum steering angle
7. front hanger surface available for Hall sensor bracket
8. front wheel spoke/brake-bolt circle diameter and bolt size
9. chosen charge connector panel cutout
10. chosen service-disconnect panel cutout
11. chosen pressure-vent panel cutout
12. finished professionally built battery module L/W/H and cable exit

**No deck drilling should be based solely on the packaging-reference CAD.**
