# Build Passport v1.3: variant-aware planning handoff

**Scope:** `NON_AUTHORITATIVE_BUILD_PASSPORT`. The Build Passport is **not** a parts order, fabrication package, assembly authorization, battery/charger specification, commissioning protocol or ride permit.

## Source of truth

- Selected candidate and priced BOM come from the existing deterministic Board Builder.
- The catalog's `sku` is retained as **SKU text**, not silently treated as a revision, exact SKU variant or current stock confirmation. `catalog_revision` remains `null` until documented.
- The dated vendor source URL, `snapshot_id` and `source.as_of` are preserved verbatim. Catalog health is also surfaced with its verification date. A dated source is not live availability.
- `manufacturer_instructions_url` stays `null` until a genuine manufacturer document for the exact revision is independently verified. Product/store URLs are never relabeled as installation manuals.
- `price.qty` is a **catalog reference price multiplier**, *not* a mechanically verified assembly or order quantity. `assembly_required_qty` remains `null` until donor inclusions and exact parts are confirmed.
- A donor listing can include hubs, bindings, deck, tires, etc. Its `included_by_donor` inventory is retained and flagged for overlap review, not blindly added to a shopping cart.
- Native currency vendor snapshots remain raw quoted text and are not silently converted to USD.
- Sourced USD snapshots and unsourced planning cost ranges are separated. A sourced price is historical, not a quote. All-in total remains `null` because shipping, tax, tools, PPE, professional labor, validation, missing prices and purchase quantities are unknown.

## Revision dependency / invalidation

A deterministic `study_identity_key` contains the exact catalog IDs and their source identity keys; it is not a cryptographic signature, certification or hardware revision guarantee. Each interface claim binds its component IDs to available source identity keys and always states `valid_for_physical_build:false`.

`proposePassportRevisionChange(passport, componentId, proposedRevision)` is a **local what-if** that:

1. Clones the passport and marks the proposed revision unverified.
2. Clears specific-revision and stock claims on the changed part.
3. Invalidates **every directly dependent** catalog interface claim containing the changed ID.
4. Updates the study identity and records the affected rule IDs and exact revalidation task.
5. Maintains `physical_qualification:NOT_QUALIFIED` and all false authority fields.

The change **does not** write to the source catalog, re-run physical tests, invent adapters, refresh vendor inventory, or grant new procurement authority. Indirect dependencies through bundled donor contents still require a later explicit dependency model and receiving inspection. That is a follow-up engineering task, not implicit clearance.

## Assembly learning path

The document separates:

1. Sources / instruction and variant review.
2. Receiving: labeling, manufacturer/part revision photos, packaging inclusions, condition and known fasteners.
3. Exact-revision mechanical measurement: mounting, axle, wheel/hub, steering sweep, brake and drive coexistence.
4. Qualified chassis assembly using verified maker-specific instructions, torque and independent inspection.
5. Battery, BMS, fuses, charger, wiring and power integration **only by a qualified specialist** after a separate electrical design and safety review.
6. Independent controlled tests and release authorization outside the generic Builder.

No live power-up steps, wiring dimensions, torque prescriptions, charging instructions or unverified manufacturer-manual links are generated by this feature.

## Validation and exports

The selected Board Builder design exposes an inspectable part table, receiving/measurement stages, source freshness, a revision-change what-if and **export passport JSON**. The print action uses an isolated print stylesheet to retain the passport without the rest of the interactive Builder.

The outputs are independently generated in Python and JavaScript. CI compares the complete JSON structure for the manual brake-first and proposed electric coexistence examples and fails on any difference:

```bash
python tools/generate_build_passport.py configurator/examples/trail_rider_profile.json brake_first_trail_core > /tmp/passport-py.json
node tools/generate_build_passport.mjs configurator/examples/trail_rider_profile.json brake_first_trail_core > /tmp/passport-js.json
python tools/compare_build_passports.py /tmp/passport-py.json /tmp/passport-js.json
```

The full suite also verifies revision invalidation, false current stock, unknown quantities, dated supplier snapshots, unpriced or speculative battery/ESC references, missing manuals and power gating.

## Future qualification before a purchase-ready kit

A separate **reviewed source refresh** must record exact variants/revisions, directly sourced manufacturer manuals, vendor-stated package quantities and inclusion/exclusion contracts. Subsequent work must produce dimensioned mechanical-interface evidence and independently reviewed safety and battery/electrical integration records before considering a separate procurement release process.

Even with sourced manuals or vendor stock confirmation, the generic software **cannot promote X1 physical authority**. The Rev-C physical release issues remain independent and unresolved until tested.
