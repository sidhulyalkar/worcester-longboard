# Rider geometry capture workflow

For dimensional fit, use a calibrated optical or structured-light scan as the primary geometry source. A multi-view phone scan is acceptable for early mock-ups if critical dimensions are checked independently.

Wi-Fi CSI pose systems are treated as dynamic pose sensors rather than dimensional metrology. Their role is repeated motion/stance validation, not final CAD dimensions.

Recommended capture set:

1. neutral standing scan
2. comfortable riding-stance scan on an unpowered mock deck
3. left foot close scan
4. right foot close scan
5. direct standing-height measurement
6. direct left/right foot length and width measurements
7. four-zone static stance load recording

Keep raw scan meshes outside version control. Reduce them to the dimensions required by CAD and controller configuration.
